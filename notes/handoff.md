# Handoff — Email 01 subject line angle

**What each group cares about:**
- Tea-curious: reassurance, simple/no-guesswork guidance.
- Gift buyers: a thoughtful, personal-feeling box, not generic.
- Daily drinkers: consistent quality and honest sourcing.

**Angle A — Calm daily ritual:** frames tea as a slow-down, settle-in moment.
**Angle B — Discovering new teas:** frames tea as something new/exciting to try.

**Pick: Angle A.** It fits "warm, calm, decent" brand voice better than B's novelty framing, and it works across all three groups without overpromising — daily drinkers get consistency, tea-curious get low-pressure reassurance, gift buyers get a calm/thoughtful feel. Angle B's "discovery" language risks hype-adjacent phrasing ("just for you," "what's next") that sits closer to the words-we-avoid list.

**Final subject line: "A quiet cup, waiting for you"** (28 characters). Chosen over the other two Angle A options because it echoes email 01's "the wait is over" theme without any pressure language, stays calm and understated, and needs no group-specific variation. Applied to the base draft and all three group copies of `01-the-wait-is-over.md`.
