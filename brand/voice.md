# Kettlebird Tea — Brand Voice Guide

For The Steep Club, our monthly tea subscription box for waitlist members.

## Our voice in three words

Warm, calm, decent.

We talk like a trustworthy friend who knows tea well — not a salesperson. No tricks, no pressure, no hype.

## Words we use

- steep, slow down, small batch, loose leaf, warm up, settle in
- thank you, welcome, glad you're here
- simple, honest, worth the wait

These fit a calm, no-nonsense brand and read as genuine rather than salesy — which builds the trust that actually drives subscriptions.

## Words we avoid

- hustle, disrupt, game-changer, limited time, act now, don't miss out
- hurry, flash sale, last chance
- "literally," "obsessed," "insane" (overhyped filler)

These create pressure or hype, which clashes with "no tricks" and can erode the trust we're building.

## Claims we must never make

- No claims of any kind — no health claims, no performance claims, no guarantees, no comparisons to competitors, no "best" or "#1" language.
- When in doubt, describe what something is (ingredients, origin, process) rather than what it does or promises.

## How we sign off

Warm and personalized, from the team — not one person's name.

Example: "Warmly, The Kettlebird Team"

Other options in the same spirit: "Steep on, The Kettlebird Team" / "With care, The Kettlebird Team"
