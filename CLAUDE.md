# Email Studio

This studio is for planning and writing email campaigns for a small business.

## Folders

- **brand/** — brand voice, logo notes, and style guidelines to keep campaigns on-brand.
- **leads/** — lists and notes about the people and businesses we might email.
- **campaigns/** — drafts and finished copy for each email campaign.
- **notes/** — ideas, meeting notes, and anything else worth keeping.

## Rules

- Write in plain English — no jargon, no corporate-speak.
- Never send email to real people. Everything here is planning and drafting only.
- Ask before deleting anything.
